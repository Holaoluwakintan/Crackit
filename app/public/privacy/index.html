Privacy policy

CrackIt: JAMB, WAEC & NECO CBT Practice · Effective 8 October 2026 (replaces the 7 October 2026 version)

CrackIt is an exam-practice app made by Olaoluwa Michael (legal name Micheal Aroja Olaoluwa, trading as Holaoluwakintan) in Nigeria. He is the data controller: the person responsible for your data. Contact: michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com.

Short version: you can use CrackIt without an account. If you sign in, we keep only what is needed to save your progress. We never sell your data, show no ads, and you can delete your data at any time.

1. Using CrackIt without an account

Your scores, streak, topic results, saved questions and settings are stored only on your phone (in the browser/app storage). We do not receive them.

2. If you sign in with Google (optional)

What we get from Google: your name, email address and profile photo. We use them only to show your account and keep your progress tied to you. We never see your Google password.

What we store: your practice progress (mock scores and history, streak days, topic results, saved and wrong questions, daily goal) and any practice packs you buy, so they work on any phone you sign in on.

Why we may use it (legal basis): you ask us to save your progress when you choose to sign in, so we process it to provide that service to you (contract) and with your consent. You can withdraw by deleting your data (section 9).

3. Payments

Optional Post-UTME practice packs cost a one-time fee shown before you pay (currently ₦700). Payments are processed by Paystack. You type your email and card or bank details into Paystack, not into CrackIt; we never see or store card details. We keep the payment reference, the pack and the amount so your purchase works on all your phones, and Paystack keeps its own payment records as the law requires. See the Terms and refund policy.

4. Admission checker

The scores and grades you type into the admission chance checker are processed on your phone and are not sent to us.

5. Question reports

If you tap “Report a problem” on a question, we receive the question ID and the note you type (and your account ID if you are signed in) so we can fix the question.

6. Who else handles your data

We do not sell or share your personal data with anyone, except these service providers that run the app for us:

  • Google: sign-in.
  • Supabase: our database (servers in the European Union).
  • Paystack: payments (Nigeria).
  • Vercel: website hosting (servers in the United States and elsewhere).

Because Supabase and Vercel store or process data outside Nigeria, your data may be transferred abroad. We only use providers that protect data with encryption and access controls, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

No ads and no advertising trackers. No collection of contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

7. How long we keep it

  • Progress and account data: until you delete it, or 2 years after your last sign-in, whichever comes first.
  • Purchase records: for as long as you keep your account, so your packs keep working.
  • Question reports: until the question is fixed, then at most 12 months.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you clear it.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, move it to another service, or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

9. Deleting your data

Signed in: open Account → Delete my CrackIt data in the app. This erases your progress and purchase records at once. Your Google sign-in record (name and email) can also be removed: email us from that address and we will delete it within 7 days. Steps are also at crackit-ng.vercel.app/delete-account.

Not signed in: clear the app's storage or use Progress → Reset progress.

10. Children

CrackIt is for students preparing for the UTME, WASSCE and NECO, usually aged 15 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you sign in or buy a pack. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's data at any time by email. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

11. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses Google with a secure code exchange (PKCE). Each account can only read its own data (the database blocks everyone else), and payment secrets stay on our server. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.

CrackIt is an independent practice app. Not affiliated with JAMB, WAEC or NECO.
