Delete your CrackIt data

You can delete everything CrackIt stores about your account at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CrackIt and tap your photo (top right) or go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete my CrackIt data and confirm.

This immediately deletes your saved progress, scores, history, saved questions and purchase records, and signs you out.

By email

Send an email from the Google address you signed in with to michaelolaoluwagab@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my CrackIt data”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. By email we also delete your sign-in record (the name and email Google gave us), so nothing about you is left.

What is deleted

  • Progress, scores, history, streaks, topic results, saved and wrong-answer lists
  • Purchase records (packs will no longer unlock on other phones)
  • By email request: your sign-in record (name and email)
  • Question reports linked to your account

Payment records that Paystack must keep by law are held by Paystack, not CrackIt.
